# Org Pilot Onboarding — One Page

> PLANET for your rescue or foster network — how the two-week pilot works.
> (内部注:本页可作邮件正文或 Notion 页发出;能力清单以 B2B-ORG-SCENARIOS-2026-09-27 §三 为准,不承诺未建功能。)

Welcome! PLANET gives every animal in your care **one shared timeline** — medications, vet visits, weights, feeding notes — that everyone involved can see and add to. Here's how your organization sets up. Setup takes about 15 minutes.

## 1. Your rescue = one shared circle ("family")

In PLANET, a *family* is any group of people caring together — for you, it's the whole rescue. Create one circle named after your organization. You (the coordinator) are its **owner** and the single point of contact for us.

## 2. Foster homes = invited members

Invite each foster home from the circle's member settings:

- **Caregiver** — can log care: completed feedings, meds given, weights, photos, symptoms. This is the role for active foster homes.
- **Viewer** — read-only, for board members or anyone who just needs to look.

An animal can be linked to **more than one circle at once** — your rescue's circle *and* the foster's own household — so fosters see the animal in their own app while you keep the organizational view. Handoffs between fosters (or duty shifts) are handled with care requests and transfers, so responsibility is never ambiguous.

## 3. Adoption day = transfer + health snapshot

When an animal is adopted:

1. **Transfer** the pet to the adopter (they accept on their phone). The complete timeline travels with the animal — the adopter inherits everything, which is the whole point.
2. **Share a vet summary** — a read-only snapshot page of the animal's health record the adopter can keep, print, or hand to their new vet. It works like an electronic passport for the animal.

## 4. Quotas — we raise them for you, manually

Normal free accounts keep 2 active pets; a rescue obviously needs more. **There is no self-signup for raised limits.** We raise them for your coordinator's account by hand, so just email support@joinplanet.pet when anything feels tight and we'll adjust it same day.

## 5. What you need (please read before starting)

- **iPhone only** — PLANET is iOS-only right now, and accounts use **Apple sign-in** (no email/password). Every active member needs an iPhone.
- The app is in **App Store review** at the moment; your pilot starts the day it's approved.
- There's no rescue dashboard or animal roster view yet — you'll use the same per-animal timeline view families use. Tell us what's missing; that's what the pilot is for.

## 6. What we ask in return

- Get **≥5 animals** onto the timeline and keep **≥2 members** active for the two weeks.
- One 15-minute debrief call at the end: what worked, what broke, what you still do in a spreadsheet.

Questions anytime: **support@joinplanet.pet**
