## 三、评论区 FAQ(提前准备好,有人问直接回)

### Q: Is this another pet social app?
**A:** No. PLANET deliberately doesn't do feeds, likes, comments, or a pet community. It solves two specific problems: coordinating daily care, and preparing complete information for the vet.

### Q: What does the AI actually do? Can it diagnose?
**A:** It does NOT diagnose. It organizes your pet's history into vet-ready summaries, surfaces patterns (e.g., "this symptom has occurred 3 times in 2 months"), and generates questions to ask your vet. It supports better care conversations, not medical decisions.

### Q: Why lifetime for only 100 people?
**A:** The first 100 believers get lifetime access for one payment. After that, PLANET moves to subscription. It's our way of rewarding early trust — and the price genuinely rises as seats fill.

### Q: Is it an app I download?
**A:** It's a web app (PWA) that works on any device. You can add it to your home screen. We're validating demand with real founding members before building native iOS/Android.

### Q: What if it doesn't work out?
**A:** Founding members get a clear refund policy. If we don't deliver what we promised, you get your money back.

### Q: 我已经有宠物保险/医院记录了,为什么还需要?
**A:** 保险记录的是理赔,医院记录的是就诊。但日常喂了什么、谁喂的药、症状从哪天开始、上次遛弯是什么时候——这些散落在聊天和记忆里。PLANET 补的就是这一块。

### Q: 几个人可以用?
**A:** 一个宠物可以邀请家人、伴侣、保姆、遛狗师共同照护。每个人看到自己该做的事。

## 五、增长杠杆

### 1. 进度条本身就是杠杆
每卖 10 个就发一条:"10/100 founding members filled. Price rises to S$69.99 after #10." 稀缺性驱动行动。

### 2. 介绍机制
创始会员邀请第二位照顾者时,这是一个天然传播点。在 onboarding 里加一句:"Share Milo's care" → 接收方看到 PLANET → 部分人成为新用户。

### 3. Vet-ready Summary 分享
用户把就医摘要分享给兽医/保姆时,接收方无需注册就能看到——这是零摩擦的产品曝光。

### 4. 创始人持续更新
在 X/即刻 上持续发"building in public"更新:这周做了什么功能、收到什么反馈、第一个付费用户说了什么。真实感是最强的冷启动武器。

## 六、不做什么(避免浪费精力)

- ❌ 不投付费广告(验证期,ROI 不明确)
- ❌ 不做 SEO(见效太慢,等 Phase 3)
- ❌ 不做宠物 KOL 合作(没预算,先用自己的狗)
- ❌ 不做"转发抽奖"(引来的是羊毛党,不是真实用户)
- ❌ 不承诺功能(卖的是结果:不漏事、就医有准备,不是功能清单)
