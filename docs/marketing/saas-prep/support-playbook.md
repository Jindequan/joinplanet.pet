# Support Playbook — 组织问询回复模板

> 用途:SAAS-PREP §P6。support@joinplanet.pet 收到组织类问询(rescue / foster network / shelter / breeder 等)时的回复模板。
> 触发关键词示例:rescue, shelter, foster network, multiple pets, organization, nonprofit, "we have X dogs/cats"。

---

## 回复模板(英文,按情况删节)

**Subject:** Re: [their subject] — PLANET for your organization

Hi [First name],

Thanks for reaching out — and for what you do. Short answer: **yes, PLANET is free, and organizations like yours can use it today.**

Here's the fit in three lines:

- **One shared timeline per animal.** Your rescue runs as one circle; foster homes join as invited members, and every med, vet visit and note lands on the animal's timeline — not in a group chat.
- **Cross-home linking.** An animal can be linked to your rescue's circle and the foster's own household at the same time, so handoffs stay clean.
- **Adoption handoff built in.** When an animal is adopted, you transfer them to the adopter and they inherit the full record, plus a shareable vet summary as their take-home paperwork.

Two honest constraints: PLANET is **iPhone-only with Apple sign-in** right now, and there's **no rescue dashboard or roster view yet** — you'd use the same per-animal timeline families use.

**Raised limits:** normal free accounts keep 2 active pets. There's no self-signup for more — we raise limits for your coordinator's account manually, at no cost. If you'd like that, just reply and we'll set you up within a day. You can read how the setup works in one page here: [org onboarding one-pager — link before sending].

And three quick questions, so we build the right thing:

1. How do foster handoffs work for you today?
2. What does an adopter take home when they adopt?
3. What software do you use now, and what does it cost?

Either way — thanks for writing.

— Devin
PLANET

## 内部纪律(回复前自查)

- **免费**:任何组织场景都不报价、不提未来收费、不谈 ARR。价值叙事 = 领养移交带来预激活家庭。
- **无自助注册**:配额调整只经人工审核 + `entitlements grant`(runbook 见 quota-runbook.md);不教用户任何绕过 C 端 2 宠限额的说法。
- **不承诺未建功能**:组织花名册、志愿者权限分层、批量导出都不存在——只描述现成能力(家庭圈/family_pet_links/transfer/就诊快照);问的人多=信号,记录下来,不顺嘴许愿。
- **三问必附**:答案按 MARKET-RESEARCH §8.4 判据归档——「spreadsheet+群、付$0」= 收入假设死;答得出软件与年费 = 深访候选。
- 附件链接占位「org onboarding one-pager」发送前须替换为本目录 org-onboarding-onepage.md 的实际链接。
